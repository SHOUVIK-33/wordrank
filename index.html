Rank of a Word

Type any word. Its letters are sorted a→z, every arrangement is listed alphabetically, and this shows the word's position in that dictionary.

Letters only · case doesn't matter · repeated letters handled

How the rank is built. At each position, count the smaller letters still unused. For each one, the rest of the word can be arranged in (remaining)! / (repeated letters)! ways — that whole block comes before our word.

Add up every such block, then add 1 for the word itself. That is its rank.
